How to read this table
r
Pearson or Spearman correlation coefficient. Ranges −1 to +1. Near zero means no linear relationship.
95 % CI
Bootstrap confidence interval. If it straddles zero the finding is not statistically distinguishable from no effect.
p (Bonf.)
p-value corrected for multiple comparisons. Values above 0.05 are not significant after correction.
pearson_within_country
Correlation after removing country-level means (demeaning). Controls for fixed country effects — a stricter test.
