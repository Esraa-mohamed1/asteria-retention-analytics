03Challenge — Findings & Associations

⚠ Observational only — correlation does not imply causation. Sample sizes are small (6 countries × limited quarters). Confounders such as sector mix, company growth phase, and local labour law are not controlled for. Treat all findings as hypothesis-generating, not causal evidence.

How to read this table
r
Pearson or Spearman correlation coefficient. Ranges −1 to +1. Near zero means no detectable linear relationship with this sample.
95% CI
Fisher z-transformed confidence interval for Pearson r. If it straddles zero the finding is not distinguishable from no effect at this sample size.
p (Bonf.)
p-value corrected for multiple comparisons (Bonferroni). Values above 0.05 are not significant after correction.
pearson_within_country
Pearson after removing country-level means (within-country demeaning). Controls for fixed country effects — a stricter test than pooled correlation.
Limitations
Only 6 countries and ≈12–20 quarters per objective. Repeated country-quarter measures inflate effective n. SENIOR_HIRE_12M skipped (cohorts of 2–3, below minimum of 8 pairs).
